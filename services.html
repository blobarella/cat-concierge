Cat Concierge®

Let your cats relax while you're away

Cat Concierge will tend to all of your feline friend's needs!

Our cat sitting service starts with a free initial consultation where we will discuss your exact requirements for you and your pets. We will tailor a package which will include:

  • Feeding, replenishing water, scooping litter boxes, clean bowls and food preparation areas
  • Administering of flea treatments and medication if required (medication in food only)
  • Playing with your cats and social interaction (if they're in the mood!), light brushing
  • Photos and daily updates sent by phone
  • All visits are for 30 minutes

To create a "lived in" look for your home while you're away, the following optional services can be provided free of charge with your cat sitting:

  • Mail collection
  • Garbage and recycling bins put out and brought in
  • Indoor and outdoor plants watered

Services for your other furry, feathery and fishy friends

Although our main focus is providing personalised services for your cats, we can also look after your other small pets either as a separate service or in conjunction with your cat care service*:

  • Fish feeding
  • Birds fed and water replaced
  • Guinea pigs, rabbits, rats or mice - fed and water replaced

Cat Vet and Groomer Transport Service

For your convenience we can also provide a cat transport service to and from your vet or grooming salon for your cat's veterinary and grooming appointments. Please contact us for pricing.

*For multiple or large cages/aquariums, exotic birds or fish, a revised fee will apply
