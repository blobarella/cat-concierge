Pricing

Initial consultation at your home:    Free of charge

Single visit for 1 cat / 2 cats:              $35 / $40

Two visits a day for 1 cat / 2 cats:     $65 / $75

Each additional cat (per visit):           $10

Key pick up or drop off fee:               $20

No fee is charged if your key is picked up at the initial consultation. Your keys can also be retained securely by Cat Concierge for your convenience for future cat sitting bookings.

Holiday Surcharge:   A Holiday surcharge applies to NSW Public Holidays, Easter and Christmas holiday periods.

Payment:   Cat Concierge accepts cash, EFT, PayPal and credit card. Bookings are not confirmed until full payment has been received.

Please refer to our Terms and Conditions. The fees listed above apply to limited suburbs within Cat Concierge's total service area. Prices will vary depending on your location, a complete price list will be provided to you at your initial consultation.
